# Discussions Guide

## Purpose
Discussions provide a place for community members to ask questions, share ideas, and exchange knowledge.

## Categories

### General
Share news, updates, and general thoughts about the project.

### Ideas
Suggest improvements and discuss possible features.

### Questions and Answers
Ask questions and help other community members.

### Show and Tell
Share your work, examples, and experiments related to the project.

## Best Practices
- Search existing discussions before creating a new topic.
- Use a clear and descriptive title.
- Stay respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Share links or examples when they help explain your point.

## Reporting Problems
Use GitHub Issues to report bugs or submit feature requests when appropriate.

## Community Participation
Everyone is encouraged to contribute, learn, and help make the project better.
